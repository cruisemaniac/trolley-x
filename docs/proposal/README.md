# Proposal & assessment briefs

Academic source material for PDE4445. The proposal is the scope/cost source of
truth; where the physical build diverges, [../../hardware/wiring.md](../../hardware/wiring.md)
and [../../hardware/bom.md](../../hardware/bom.md) record the as-built reality.

- `PDE4445_TrolleyX_Proposal_Revised_v2.pdf` - the revised dissertation proposal.
- `PDE4445_ModuleHandbook.pdf` - module rules (assessment weights, academic
  integrity, Gen-AI policy, ethics).
- `PDE4445_2526_Introduction.pdf`, `PDE4445_2526_ResearchQuestion.pdf`,
  `PDE4445_2526_Blog.pdf` - assignment briefs.
- `build-plan.md` - the team's early sim-to-real plan. **Note:** its
  vision-follow approach and MDDS30/2-motor drivetrain are superseded (see ADR 0001);
  its timeline, team split, and assessment mapping remain useful.
